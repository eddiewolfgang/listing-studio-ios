## Information stored in the app

Listing Studio stores the item titles, prices, condition, pickup areas, notes, descriptions, tracking status and selected photos that you enter. In this version these are stored locally inside the app on your iPhone. We do not receive this inventory through an app-operated server.

The app does not require an account, contain advertising or analytics SDKs, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

## Selected photos

You choose which images to import using Apple’s photo picker. The app stores reduced-size copies of the selected images when you save a listing. The original library images are unchanged. Imported copies are re-encoded without retaining the original EXIF metadata. Original photos you separately select directly in another app are governed by that app’s behavior.

## Copying, sharing and external services

Copying puts the selected text on this device’s clipboard. Sharing sends the content you choose to the destination you select in the iOS share sheet. Opening Facebook Marketplace or our support/policy website navigates to an external service. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy practices. Listing Studio does not collect your Facebook credentials or publish listings automatically.

## Backups and retention

Listings remain in local app storage until you delete them or remove the app. Your Apple device backups may include this data according to your backup settings. The app does not itself provide cross-device synchronization. Backups are managed separately through Apple.

## Deletion

Open a saved listing and select Delete listing to remove its record and saved photo copies from the app. Removing the app removes its local storage. Manage any retained device backups through your Apple settings. Deletion here does not remove anything that you previously shared with another service.
