## Description

Keep your next sale organized with Listing Studio.

Add an item’s details, asking price, condition and pickup area. Turn those facts into an editable description, arrange your photos, and keep your drafts ready whenever you have time to post.

• Save item descriptions and up to 10 photos per listing.
• Reorder photos and choose a cover.
• Copy your title and description separately.
• Share text and photos using your iPhone’s sharing tools.
• Track drafts, listed items and completed sales.
• Keep your inventory on your iPhone without creating an account.

Listing Studio prepares your content. You still create, review and publish your listing in Facebook Marketplace yourself. The app does not automatically publish listings, synchronize Marketplace changes, or handle buyer messages, transactions or payments.

Your listings are stored on this iPhone. This version does not synchronize with other devices or the web app. Device backup behavior depends on your Apple settings.

Listing Studio is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta or Facebook.

## Review notes

No login is required. The app is a native local inventory and listing preparation utility. It is not a marketplace, broker, payment service or web wrapper.

To review:
1. Tap + and add an item title, price, condition and notes.
2. Import photos using Add photos, then use a photo’s menu to reorder or remove it.
3. Tap Assemble from item details; edit the description.
4. Save. Close and reopen the app to verify local persistence.
5. Open the listing and try Copy title, Copy description and Share text and photos.
6. Set Listed or Sold and save. These statuses are local tracking labels.
7. About provides the final privacy and support links once configured.

The Facebook button is an external navigation to its listing-creation page. Facebook authentication, availability and publication occur outside this app. No Facebook API or credentials are used. Copy/share can also be used without opening Facebook.
